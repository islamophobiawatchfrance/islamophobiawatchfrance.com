News
17 November 2026 · 3 min read

Municipal police officers in Carcassonne have been found making racist, misogynist and seditious remarks in a six-hour bodycam recording from November 6, 2025, according to Mediapart's review of an investigation report commissioned by the city of Carcassonne. The recording, revealed publicly by Midi libre on September 8, documents statements made by a patrol unit, with particular focus on remarks attributed to a senior officer identified as brigadier-chef principal César R., 50 years old.

What happened

Mediapart obtained the investigation report that the city of Carcassonne commissioned following discovery of the officers' remarks. According to Mediapart's account, the report failed to establish responsibility for any of the documented statements, which allowed the mayor to contain the matter rather than pursue it further.

The bodycam footage was recorded during a routine municipal police patrol on November 6, 2025. When Midi libre published extracts from the six-hour recording on September 8, the revelations triggered what Mediapart describes as a scandal that "struck Carcassonne like a storm." The footage documents what Mediapart characterizes as "a litany of racist, misogynist and seditious remarks" made by the patrol unit.

Reaction

Mediapart has not yet published the full details of community or institutional responses to the scandal, as the investigation report's contents remain under subscriber restriction on the Mediapart website.

Why it matters

The case reflects ongoing concerns in France about accountability within police forces and the treatment of investigations into officer misconduct. The apparent failure of the internal investigation to establish responsibility—despite documented evidence on bodycam footage—raises questions about how municipal police departments in France handle complaints of discriminatory conduct and whether such inquiries are conducted with sufficient rigor to satisfy transparency demands from affected communities and civil society.

Background

Carcassonne is a municipality in the Aude département in southern France. Municipal police forces in France (police municipale) are distinct from the national police (police nationale) and are employed by local governments to handle community policing and local security matters. Bodycam footage has become an increasingly common form of evidence in investigations of police conduct across France in recent years. Mediapart is an independent French investigative journalism outlet that has published numerous investigations into police misconduct and institutional accountability.

Q&A

Who is César R. and what specifically did he say?
According to Mediapart, César R. is a 50-year-old senior municipal police officer identified as brigadier-chef principal. Mediapart states he made remarks documented in the bodycam footage, but the full content of his specific statements remains behind Mediapart's subscriber paywall and has not been publicly detailed beyond characterization as racist, misogynist and seditious.
Why did the initial investigation fail to establish responsibility?
Mediapart does not provide specific details about the investigation's methodology or reasoning, only that the commissioned report "established no responsibility," thereby enabling the mayor to "bury the story." The full findings remain restricted to Mediapart subscribers.
What action has been taken against the officers involved?
Mediapart's available reporting does not detail any disciplinary measures, suspensions, or criminal referrals against the officers involved.
When was the investigation commissioned and completed?
Mediapart does not specify the timeline for when the city commissioned the investigation or when it was completed, only that the report's findings are now under review.

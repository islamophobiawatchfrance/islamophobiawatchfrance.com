Islamophobia Watch France's top story

In Aix, three students suffer a racist assault by far-right groups

Three students were attacked in a racially motivated assault by far-right activists on Saturday, October 10, in Aix-en-Provence, France, according to reporting by Marsactu. All three victims filed complaints with police. The incident occurred near the Faculty of Letters on the outskirts of an antifascist demonstration taking place in the city.

What happened

Around 3:20 p.m. on October 10, Sofian and his colleague Adan stopped in front of the Faculty of Letters in Aix-en-Provence while waiting for a third colleague who held keys to Adan's student housing in the university residence. Sofian told Marsactu that he sensed they were being followed. As they passed a notice board displaying Marine Le Pen campaign posters, approximately fifteen people approached them in a threatening manner, according to Sofian's account to police during his formal complaint.

An independent monitoring project tracking Islamophobia and celebrating Muslim life in France — for English-speaking audiences around the world.

France has the highest proportion of Muslim citizens in the entirety of Europe. What happens in France matters; it can be either a guiding light or a warning sign for other European countries and other European Muslims. Islamophobia Watch France is a way for English-speaking audiences to understand what is happening in the world of French Muslims, and to peel back the lid on the media and Islamophobic leaders who fuel anti-Muslim hatred. We shine a light on the problem of Islamophobia in France, while also ensuring we highlight the array of achievements, big and small, of France's Muslims.
